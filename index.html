History

FC Cincinatti was founded in 2015, and after promotion to Major League Soccer in 2019, they started growing a huge fanbase. For the 2020-21 MLS season, they used the Addidas Heritage Link kit.

They finished that season in 14th place with a record of 4 wins, 4 draws, and 15 losses. You have to give them sympathy because they just joined a higher, competitive league. When they started the 2021-22 season, they featured the Dynamic Kit.

They finished the 2021-22 season in 5th place, which is pretty good jumping up from 9 places in the standings. A really memorable part of the season was the new hiring of the coach, Pat Noonan. They went to 5th place and a record of 12-9-13. This got them to their first ever playoffs in their history. When they started their 2022-23 season, they featured the Juncta Juvant kit.

Listen to me, because this is their golden era. FC Cincinatti got 1st place in their Conference and 1st place in the overall MLS rankings, or first in the whole league, and they had a record of 20-5-9. They went on and won the Supporter's Shield trophy, which is their first major trophy in their history. It is going to take me years just to describe each season and put in their kit for that specific season and their standings and record, so let me move on.

TQL Stadium

TQL Stadium opened on May 16, 2021 on the West End. It holds 26,000 fans, making it one of the largest soccer-specific stadiums in the MLS. The outer shell of the stadium has 500 LED lights that flash orange and blue animations at nighttime. A 360 degree canopy is rested on the roof to protect the fans and trapping the chants inside the stadium. In fact, it was named Best Venue at the 2022 World Summit in Madrid and 2022 Prix Versailles World Title in Sports.

Matchday Traditions

In the 3.5 hours before kickoff, all the fans gather at the Washington Park in Over-The-Rhine for food, live music, family activities, and lawn games. An hour before kickoff, groups gather to the Washington Park for something called March to the Match. These is when everyone marches to the stadium with flags and banners and safe blue and orange handheld fog machines. The mascot of the club is named Gary, which is the winged lion on the crest of the club. Then, before the match starts, a special guest or local hero will pull a sword out of a pedestal to hype up the crowd.
